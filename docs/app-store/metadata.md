**Description**

ishler is a small task app for people whose tasks get lost between notes, calendars and ten different lists.

Just write what you need to do:

"Send the proposal to Acme by Friday x"
"Call Deniz tomorrow 5:30pm"
"every monday weekly plan"

ishler works out the person, the task, the date and the time, puts it on your list and reminds you when it matters.

• One sentence, no forms. Dates, times, repeats and people are understood in English and Turkish, entirely on your device.
• Categories with shortcuts. End a note with a letter or digit (x, p, 1) or in parentheses, and it lands in the right category, in its own colour.
• Recurring tasks. "Send the invoice on the 15th": it puts itself back on your list every month, with its checklist.
• Reminders that don't nag. The evenings before a deadline, the morning of, and a nudge only if you have been away. Mark done or snooze right from the notification.
• Focus screen. One task at a time, big and clear.
• Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen.
• Apple Calendar, Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight search, CSV export.
• Syncs between your devices through your own iCloud. There is no account and no server in between.

ishler is free and open source: github.com/ruzgarselvan/ishler

## App Review notes
No sign-in is needed. The app asks for Calendar access to show the user's events next to their tasks and to add dated tasks to the calendar, and for notification permission to show reminders. Sync uses the user's own iCloud private database (CloudKit); iCloud sign-in is optional and the app works fully offline without it.
